# Crashpot

A party of tiny games for 2 to 6 players. No talking needed.

**Play:** https://fardasticmanguy-rgb.github.io/cool-game/

## How it works

1. Tap **Quick play** (or Host) and get your friends into the lobby.
2. The host picks 4, 6 or 9 games and taps Start.
3. Before each game, a box explains the rules with a countdown. Then the game starts.
4. Win games to get points. Most points at the end wins.

## إزاي تلعب

1. دوس **Quick play** وخلي صحابك يدخلوا اللوبي.
2. الهوست يختار ٤ أو ٦ أو ٩ ألعاب ويدوس Start.
3. قبل كل لعبة، في مربع بيشرح القواعد وعداد. بعدها اللعبة تبدأ.
4. اكسب الألعاب عشان تاخد نقط. اللي معاه أكتر نقط في الآخر يكسب.

## 🎰 Casino (open floor, fake chips)

Just for fun. No real money is ever used.

The casino is an open floor. Everyone starts with 1,000 chips and walks between tables whenever they want. The host picks how long it runs (5, 8 or 12 minutes). Most chips when the clock hits 0 wins.

| Table | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 🚀 Crash | **Live, everyone rides the same rocket.** Bet before it launches, cash out before it blows. Set an auto cash out if you're scared |
| 💣 Mines | Pick 1, 3, 5 or 10 bombs. Every safe tile grows your prize. Cash out any time |
| 🔻 Plinko | Drop balls down the pegs. The edges pay up to 13× |
| 🎰 Slots | Spin and match the symbols. 💎💎💎 pays 25× |
| 🎡 Roulette | Bet on 🔴 or ⚫ (×2) or 🟢 (×14) and watch the wheel |
| 🃏 Blackjack | Hit, stand or double. Blackjack pays 3 to 2 |
| 🔼 Higher or Lower | Guess the next card. The riskier the guess, the bigger the boost |
| 🎲 Over / Under 7 | Will two dice add up under, over, or exactly 7? |
| ⚔️ Duel a friend | Challenge someone to 🪙 Coin Flip, 🃏 High Card or 🎲 Dice Duel. Winner takes the bet |

- Everyone's balance updates live, and you can see which table each player is at
- A live feed shows big wins, crash cash outs and duel results
- Out of chips? Ask the house for 300
- Bots play the tables too, and sometimes challenge you to duels

## 🔐 Private chat

Tap the faint little **🔐** in the bottom-right corner to open [Chat](https://fardasticmanguy-rgb.github.io/chat/) in a side panel, right inside the game.

- Type a room code to join, just like on the Chat site. Messages are encrypted with the code
- **— (minimize)** hides the panel but keeps you connected. New notes show a small red dot on the 🔐
- **🔒 (close)** locks it. Nothing is saved on the device: no room codes, no history. Next time you open it you type the code again
- **🎮 Invite** sends your current lobby to the chat room, so friends can tap Join
- Tap **Join** on a game invite in the chat and you go straight into that lobby

## Extras

- Emoji reactions to trash-talk without talking. With Family friendly off, 💬 adds the Arabic phrases
- **Family friendly** mode is on by default
- Bots can fill empty seats
- **Teams**: turn Teams on in Lobby options (Party, One game, Casino), tap 🔴 Join Red or 🔵 Join Blue. The team with the best average wins
- Every game shows a one-line "how to play" in English and Arabic, on the intro and while you play
- Works great with just 2 players: the loser of a round gets 0, so every round counts
- **Practice vs bots** works offline
- **Jump in mid-game**: open a lobby that is already playing (or tap Jump in) and you join at the next round. Late joiners start with the lowest score (1,000 chips in Casino). If the room is full, a bot gives up its seat. Locked lobbies stay locked
- **Online now** shows who else has the game open, and which lobby they're in
- **Invite** anyone online from your lobby, or tap **Share link** to paste it in the chat
- **Refresh-proof lobbies**: refresh as the host and your lobby, players and game come back. Guests who refresh rejoin on their own
- Fonts ship with the game, so they work on school Wi-Fi, Firefox and tablets

## Play links

`?play=<code>` opens or joins that lobby (2 to 12 letters or numbers). Add `&mode=cards|brawl|hide|party|single|casino`, or `&game=rocket` (any mini game) to start in One game mode with it. `&games=rocket,mines,whack` picks the Party games.
